# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want LabLedger to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, identity or affiliation. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good community:

- Being kind, patient and respectful in issues, reviews and discussions.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assuming good faith and asking questions before judging.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that
do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban any
contributor for behaviour they deem inappropriate. To report an issue, contact
the maintainers via a private channel; reports will be reviewed and handled
confidentially.
