How each method behaves
MethodSubtractsBest for
Nonenothingclean, stable baselines; keeps raw trace
Solvent blankblank spectrum (per point)sloped solvent absorbance

Endpoint and auto-zero derive from a blank run, never from the sample itself — so the correction is concentration-independent and cannot bend the calibration.

Why pick peaks again

A recorded run keeps every detected peak. Re-picking on the result page re-analyzes that stored measurement at another λ — quantitation is re-scaled by the ε-ratio, the calibration is refit, and the original judgment stays untouched for audit. The chain hash records the change.

Here in the console, picking a peak only sets the next run's active wavelength.

Result is judged against acceptance criteria and compared with ground truth; misses are logged to model integrity.
